# Dark Matter

## Verdict

6/10. Enjoyable enough, but it felt like a reheated version of `Recursion`: familiar conceptually and less fresh. The one-sentence-per-line prose style was actively annoying.

## What worked

- Fast, accessible thriller pacing
- Still enjoyable as a high-concept read

## What did not

- Felt conceptually derivative after `Recursion`
- Did not offer much that felt genuinely new
- One-sentence-per-line presentation felt like LinkedIn-post writing and became very irritating

## Reader comments

> Felt like a microwaved Recursion. Enjoyable, but nothing very new, conceptually. The LinkedIn post style with one sentence per line was very, very annoying.
>
> 6/10

## Recommendation implications

### Recommend more of

- High-concept thrillers only when the central mechanism feels genuinely fresh
- Cinematic pacing that still uses normal, unobtrusive prose

### Do not assume

- Enjoying one book by an author means a similar earlier variation will land equally well
- Fast prose is automatically transparent prose

### Largest risk in similar books

- Familiar concepts and aggressively fragmented formatting can expose the machinery of a thriller instead of immersing the reader.
