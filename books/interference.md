# Interference

## Verdict

9/10. A strong continuation of `Semiosis`. The clash between Pax and humans from Earth was especially interesting, and the world remained convincing and immersive.

## What worked

- The collision between Pax's evolved society and Earth humans
- Continued depth in the social and ecological systems established in `Semiosis`
- A believable extension of the first book's world rather than a simple repetition
- Strong continuation of the series' causal worldbuilding

## What did not

- The discovery of the other continent felt underexplored
- The book opened an especially interesting part of Pax but did not go as deeply into it as desired

## Reader comments

> Second book is still a solid 9/10, the clash with earth humans is really interesting, but I wish it had gone a bit deeper into the other continent discovery.

## Recommendation implications

### Recommend more of

- Sequels that deepen an established world through cultural collision
- Exploration that adds genuinely new ecological or social dimensions to an already credible setting
- Stories willing to stay with a fascinating place long enough to understand it deeply

### Avoid

- Introducing major new regions or systems without giving them enough time to become fully realized
